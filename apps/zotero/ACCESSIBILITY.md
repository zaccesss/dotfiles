# Accessibility

Larger text and a reading surface matched to the Zacess theme in use.

## Vision

- The interface font is 15 percent larger than Zotero's default and notes are 16 points.
- The high contrast Zacess themes use Zotero's black reader page, so PDFs read as light text on black. Cream uses the sepia page for a softer light surface.
- Zotero's own dark and light modes carry the interface, so every control keeps Zotero's tested contrast and focus outlines.

## Known gap

A Zacess colour scheme for the PDF reader is not possible yet: custom reader themes live in Zotero's synced database rather than its settings.
